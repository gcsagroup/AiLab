# Ver2.4（021）Execution Progress

The current fixed company-signed candidate is Ver2.4（021）. The issue where the manual-revision dialog moved the background after reaching a scroll boundary has completed a source fix, a before-and-after development-browser comparison, and local native acceptance. The two blank frames previously seen in 020 were not reproduced in this round, and their root cause remains undetermined. This round does not sign off complete long-list rendering, keyboard paths, AC-16/23/27, or M0—M6.

The fixed App path remains `apps/desktop/src-tauri/target/release/bundle/macos/GCSA AI Lab.app`, and the signing organization is Global Cybersecurity Alliance Limited.

Read-only reproduction in 020 confirmed that reaching the dialog’s bottom could move the background.
